MARKETING STRATEGY & AI ADOPTION

A clear strategy.
Before another tool.

AI marketing strategy connects your business goals, customer needs, and marketing priorities with specific uses of AI. The starting point is not which tool to buy. It is which problem is worth solving.

Discuss your business ↗

Who this is for

For business owners, founders, and marketing leaders who want to adapt to AI without scattering their budget across disconnected experiments.

What should change in your marketing?

New tools can create more output without making your marketing more effective. Before introducing AI, we look at your offer, target customers, positioning, and the journey from discovery to enquiry or purchase. We identify what is already working and where the friction actually sits.

That review distinguishes strategic problems from execution problems. Unclear positioning needs a sharper message; a repetitive reporting task may need a simpler process or automation. Treating both as an AI-tool problem can make the work more complicated, not more useful.

A practical roadmap, not a tool shopping list

We map potential applications against their expected value, effort, data requirements, and risks. Options may include customer research, content briefing, creative testing, lead follow-up, or reporting. Each opportunity needs a reason to exist, an accountable owner, and a realistic way to evaluate it.

  • A review of marketing goals, positioning, channels, and current workflows
  • A prioritized set of AI opportunities, including what not to automate
  • An assessment of existing tools before adding new subscriptions
  • A focused pilot brief with a baseline, success measures, and review points
  • An agreed roadmap with responsibilities and implementation dependencies

What a first pilot could look like

For example, a team struggling with inconsistent content briefs could test a reusable briefing workflow before considering a new publishing platform. The pilot might compare preparation time, editing effort, factual accuracy, and brand consistency with the current process. This is an example of an engagement, not a claimed client result.

The point is to make a decision based on real work. If the pilot adds more review effort than it removes, that is useful information. We refine the workflow, change the approach, or decide not to expand it.

How we define success

Success measures depend on your starting point. They may include qualified enquiries, conversion quality, time spent on a task, or the amount of rework a process creates. We separate operational improvements from revenue claims and document the limits of what the data can tell us.

A strategy engagement can stand alone or lead into implementation support and team enablement. The deliverables, timeline, fee, and responsibilities are agreed before work begins.

Questions about this service

Do we need an established marketing strategy first?+

No. If the fundamentals need attention, we start there. AI should support a clear marketing direction rather than cover up gaps in positioning or customer understanding.

Will you choose tools for us?+

Tool evaluation can be part of the work, but it follows the use case and requirements. Existing tools, data handling, integration needs, cost, and human review all inform the recommendation.

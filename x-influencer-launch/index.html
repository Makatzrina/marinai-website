WHY LAUNCHES ON X FALL FLAT

Posting isn’t launching.
Reach needs the right voices.

01 / THE WRONG ACCOUNTS

Big numbers, empty rooms.

Follower counts are easy to inflate. Paying for an audience that isn’t real, or isn’t yours, is the fastest way to waste a launch budget.

02 / THE WRONG MESSAGE

Posts that read like ads.

A brief copied from your pitch deck produces posts nobody shares. The angle has to work in a feed, in the influencer’s own voice.

03 / THE MISSING LOOP

No idea what worked.

Launch day ends, the posts scroll away, and nobody can say which voice or which angle moved people. The next launch starts from zero.

THE INFLUENCER SIDE

The right voices, briefed and paid.

  • Find the influencers. Accounts that match your market, checked for real audiences, genuine engagement and recent views.
  • Write their briefs. Clear key points, what to avoid and the disclosure rules, with room for each influencer’s own voice.
  • Handle payments. Terms, scheduling, follow-ups and payment, so your team never chases a single invoice.
YOUR SIDE

A story worth posting.

  • Find the key messages. The angles that make your launch worth talking about, tested against how your audience actually speaks.
  • Write the script. A launch video script built for the feed: a strong first three seconds, one clear idea, one next step.
  • Create the video. Your launch video, produced and ready for you and the influencers to share.
AFTER THE LAUNCH

Learn, then launch smarter.

  • Report the results. Views, engagement and clicks per post where they can be tracked.
  • Analyse what worked. Which accounts and angles performed, which underdelivered, and why.
  • Plan the next one. Clear recommendations, so your next launch starts from evidence, not guesses.

HOW IT WORKS

Five steps.

  1. 01

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions about
X launches.

Something else on your mind? Ask it on the call.

How much does an X influencer launch cost?+

There are two parts. The influencer fees depend on who is booked and usually run from $5,000 to $30,000 for a launch. MarinAI’s own services are priced per item, so you only pay for what you need. Use the calculator on this page for an estimate; the final quote is agreed after a call.

How do you choose the influencers?+

By fit, not follower count. Each account is checked for a real, relevant audience, genuine engagement, recent views and past brand work. You get a shortlist with the reasoning for each account and approve it before anything is booked.

Do I approve the content before it goes live?+

Yes. You approve the key messages, the influencer shortlist, the briefs, the script and the video. Influencers keep their own voice, so posts sound like them, within the brief you signed off.

Are the influencer posts disclosed as paid?+

Yes. Every paid post is clearly marked as paid, for example with an #ad tag. Disclosure protects your brand and the influencer, and audiences trust it more than hidden promotion.

Can I choose only some of the services?+

Yes. Pick the parts you need in the calculator. Some teams already have a video or a message and only need the influencer side; others want the full launch handled from start to finish.

What do I get after the launch?+

A report on what happened: views, engagement and clicks per post where they can be tracked, which accounts and angles performed, which underdelivered, and what to change next time.

Can you guarantee results?+

No one can honestly guarantee views or sign-ups on X. What you get is a launch planned around clear goals, influencers chosen for fit, every post tracked, and an honest read of the results.

How do we start?+

Book a call. Bring your launch date, what you’re launching and a rough budget. On the call we agree the goal, the scope and the timeline, and you get a written proposal before any work starts.

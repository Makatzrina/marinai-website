How much does an X influencer launch cost?+

It depends on what you need and which influencers you book. Influencer fees usually run from $5,000 to $30,000 for a launch. Tick what you need on this page, book a call, and you get a clear quote before any work starts.

How do you choose the influencers?+

By fit, not follower count: a real, relevant audience, genuine engagement and recent views. You approve the shortlist before anyone is booked.

Do I approve everything before it goes live?+

Yes. The messages, the influencer shortlist, the briefs, the script and the video all come to you for sign-off.

Are the influencer posts disclosed as paid?+

Yes. Every paid post is clearly marked as paid, for example with an #ad tag.

Can I choose only some of the services?+

Yes. Tick only what you need. Some teams already have a video and only need the influencer side.

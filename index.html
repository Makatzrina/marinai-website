GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Before we

Clarity is a good place to begin.

What does an AI marketing consultant do?+

An AI marketing consultant connects business and marketing goals with practical uses of AI. At MarinAI, that means reviewing your current marketing, choosing useful applications, defining human oversight, and helping your team test and adopt a better way of working.

Who is MarinAI for?+

MarinAI is for business owners, founders, and marketing teams that want a clearer strategy for adapting to AI. A useful starting point is a specific challenge: unclear priorities, changing search visibility, repetitive work, or a team unsure how to use AI consistently.

Can you help if we already have a marketing team or agency?+

Yes. The consulting can complement your existing team or agency rather than replace it. We can focus on strategic priorities, a workflow pilot, search visibility, or practical working sessions. Responsibilities and scope are agreed before the work starts.

What is the difference between SEO, GEO, and AEO?+

SEO focuses on visibility in search engines. GEO focuses on how a business is understood and referenced in generative AI responses. AEO focuses on clear answers to customer questions. They overlap: accessible pages, useful content, identifiable expertise, and credible evidence support all three.

Can you guarantee a ranking or a mention in ChatGPT?+

No. Search rankings and AI-generated answers are controlled by external systems and vary by query, time, and context. The work focuses on improving your foundations and measuring observable changes, not promising a first position or guaranteed citation.

Do we need to buy new AI tools?+

Not necessarily. We start by reviewing your current tools, tasks, and constraints. A better brief or simpler workflow may be more useful than another subscription. New tools should have a clear purpose, appropriate data safeguards, and a way to assess their value.

How do we start, and what does consulting cost?+

Start by emailing Marina with your website and the marketing challenge you want to solve. The scope, deliverables, responsibilities, timeline, and fee are agreed before work begins. There is no consulting subscription or payment taken through this website.

Is Commex available as a live service?+

Commex is currently an interactive preview of a product by MarinAI. You can explore its conversation-discovery and reply-review experience, but live discovery, payments, and publishing are not enabled. It is separate from Marina’s consulting services.

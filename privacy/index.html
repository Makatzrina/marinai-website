Website privacy.

This notice covers the public MarinAI consulting pages and explains the current website setup. Commex is a separate product preview linked from this site.

Who to contact

MarinAI is the consulting brand of Marina Katz-Goldenberg. For questions about information you share, or to request access, correction, or deletion, email marketing@marinai.xyz. The business is registered in Portugal.

Visiting this website

The consulting pages do not set analytics or advertising cookies, run third-party tracking scripts, or load social-media embeds. The pages use system fonts and locally hosted assets. Our host, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as IP addresses for security and service delivery. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Contacting Marina

The email links open your email application. No contact form sends your details through this website. If you choose to email, the information you include is used to respond to and discuss your enquiry. Please do not send passwords, API keys, confidential client data, or payment details. You can contact Marina to discuss or request deletion of enquiry information, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations.

External services

LinkedIn and other external links take you to services with their own privacy practices. No LinkedIn widget is embedded here. This site does not enrol visitors in a mailing list or take consulting payments.

Commex preview

The Commex preview keeps form selections in browser memory for the current session. It does not submit a brand brief, take payment, search X, or publish replies. Refreshing resets the preview. Commex currently loads Google Fonts, so its requests differ from the consulting pages. A future live service will require its own updated privacy information before collecting customer data.

Your rights

If applicable to your situation, data-protection rights can include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability. You may also raise a concern with a data-protection authority; in Portugal, this is the CNPD.

TEAM ENABLEMENT & ONGOING ADVISORY

Better tools need
confident people.

AI enablement helps a marketing team develop shared, practical ways of using AI. It combines task-specific guidance, clear boundaries, and repeatable review habits so new tools support the work rather than create more uncertainty.

Consulting with Marina Katz-Goldenberg · MarinAI
Discuss your business ↗

Who this is for

For business owners and marketing leaders whose teams are experimenting with AI but need a consistent approach, or who want an outside perspective on changing marketing priorities.

Move beyond individual experiments

One person may use AI to prepare a brief while another uses it to draft content or summarize research. Without shared expectations, the team can end up with uneven quality, repeated work, and uncertainty about what can be trusted or shared.

We look at the real tasks people perform and identify where a common approach would help. The emphasis is on judgment and repeatability: what information to provide, how to evaluate an output, when to verify it, and when not to use AI.

Practical sessions around real marketing work

Working sessions can be scoped around content planning, audience research, campaign briefs, reporting, or a specific workflow. We use appropriate examples and agree what information can safely be included.

  • Role-specific use cases connected to marketing priorities
  • Reusable briefing structures and prompt patterns
  • Fact-checking, source review, and brand-voice checks
  • Guidance on data handling and appropriate human approval
  • Documentation your team can revisit and adapt

Advisory support for the decisions that follow

Ongoing support can provide a regular place to review priorities, assess tools, and discuss what the team is learning. We distinguish promising experiments from distractions and connect those decisions back to the business goal.

This is not a promise to be available at all times or to manage every channel. The meeting cadence, response expectations, responsibilities, and any hands-on support are defined in the engagement scope. Your existing team or agency can remain responsible for execution.

What progress looks like

Confidence alone is not enough. We can evaluate whether people use the agreed process, whether briefs become clearer, whether review effort changes, and whether the team knows when to escalate an uncertain result. The right measures depend on the tasks being improved.

The aim is to leave the team with a useful working method rather than dependence on a collection of one-off prompts. That method should be understandable, documented, and adaptable as the tools change.

Choose the right starting point

A focused session may be enough for one recurring task. A broader adoption challenge may need an initial strategy review and a pilot before training. We discuss the gap first and agree an appropriate format rather than selling a standard package regardless of need.

Questions about this service

Is this suitable for a non-technical team?+

Yes. The focus is practical marketing work and informed judgment, not learning to code. Any technical dependencies are identified and assigned as part of the scope.

Can you work alongside our agency?+

Yes. Advisory can complement an existing agency or internal team. We agree responsibilities and communication so the engagement adds clarity rather than another layer of conflicting instructions.
